**El Dorado Launcher** for **Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** on PC. **1.0.0**: the first stable release.

Players of a beta: the launcher offers this update by itself (Settings → LAUNCHER → Check for updates), and keeps
your saves and settings. New players: extract the zip anywhere, start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and choose your
game folder (the one with eboot.bin and sce_sys). No game files are included: you play your own copy (update 1.02
is recommended: most fixes and the cheats need it).

## Highlights

- 🗺️ **One launcher for the three games, in 22 languages** (Arabic right to left), each game in its own color.
- ⏸️ **The game stands still in the background:** minimized or Alt+Tabbed, everything stops (time, enemies, sound),
  and coming back opens the game's pause menu.
- 💥 **When a game crashes or freezes**, the launcher shows why, with **PLAY AGAIN** and **PROBLEM REPORT**, also
  for games started from Steam or a Desktop shortcut.
- 🎮 **Each game in Steam** with El Dorado's own icon and library pictures; an open Steam is closed and opened again
  for you.
- 📈 **Smoother play, measured on your PC:** after a session slowed by the graphics card, one step lower upscaling
  quality is offered (only on your yes).
- ⚡ **Prepare shaders before you play:** the first start opens straight away, and the cache is never lost on exit.
- 🎯 **Cheats for all three games**, and **Damage to Nate**: play Crushing while Nate takes damage as on Easy.
- 🌏 **El Dorado's in-game menu in your language**, Korean and Traditional Chinese included.

## New since Beta 1

- The game stands still while its window is minimized or out of focus, and opens its pause menu when you are back.
- The crash screen (PLAY AGAIN, PROBLEM REPORT), from the launcher, Steam and Desktop shortcuts.
- Add the three games to Steam: each with its own icon and pictures; Steam is closed and opened again for you.
- The frame rate advice after a session slowed by the graphics card.
- The window fits your screen; **F11** switches between a window and fullscreen while you play.
- Cutscenes no longer stick on one picture while their sound goes on.
- Uncharted 1's turn-the-wheel prompt on the keyboard in the launcher's look.
- **Give all treasures** once per save: a new playthrough gets them too.
- The in-game menu in Korean and Traditional Chinese (with Windows' own fonts).
- The launcher's version under its name in the side bar; its buttons work right away at start.
- The number pad's **Del** deletes a save like **Delete** does.

## The launcher

- **A home screen** for mouse, keyboard and controller: each game with where you left off, your play time, last
  play, average FPS and trophy progress, and a big PLAY.
- **22 languages**, and every game language your copy holds, read from your game files (a fan translation shows in
  the place of the language it replaced).
- **Players:** named profiles, each with its own saves.
- **Settings** per game (display, performance) or for all games (controls, language, game files, launcher,
  cheats); one set of controls for the three games, with each game's own keys where you want them.
- **Saves, Trophies and Gallery** pages; backups of every changed save before each start.
- **Problem report** (Settings → LAUNCHER) and **Check El Dorado.bat** when something goes wrong.

## In the games

- **A fast engine** with DLSS, FSR 3.1, FSR 4, XeSS and frame generation.
- **Straight to the main menu**, with loading and shader preparation screens in the launcher's look.
- **El Dorado's in-game menu** (Insert, or L3 + R3 on a controller) with an FPS / GPU / CPU / RAM / VRAM counter,
  checkpoints and save slots.
- **Key prompts** drawn as keyboard keys in the game's colors, in English (US) or any keyboard layout of your
  Windows.
- **The close button and Alt+F4 ask first**, so a slip of the hand keeps your progress.
- **Saves** with a picture of the moment they were made.
- **Mouse on mounted guns** (Uncharted 1's jeep gun and the turrets of all three games).
- **Cheats:** infinite ammo and grenades, no reload, one hit kills, all treasures, every reward and chapter, and
  **Damage to Nate**.

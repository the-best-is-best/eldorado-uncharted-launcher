**El Dorado Launcher** for **Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** on PC.

Extract the zip anywhere, start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and choose your game folder (the one with
eboot.bin and sce_sys). No game files are included: you play your own copy.

## El Dorado 1.0.0

- **A fast engine.** Draws are prepared on several threads and the build is profile-optimized (the
  Uncharted 1 jungle runs at about 52 FPS on an RTX 3060 Laptop, 1080p, DLSS Quality). The first start of each
  game prepares its shaders once.
- **A new home screen**, made for a controller as much as for a mouse: each game with where you left off,
  your play time, last play, average FPS and trophy progress, and a big PLAY.
- **Recommended preset:** the launcher reads your GPU and picks the settings for it on the first start (DLSS
  on RTX, FSR 4 on Radeon RX 9000). DLSS, FSR 3.1, FSR 4 and **frame generation** (FSR 3).
- **El Dorado's in-game menu** (Insert, or L3 + R3 / L2 + R3 on a controller) with a counter that can show
  FPS, CPU, GPU, RAM and VRAM. **Quit** is added to each game's own menus.
- **Key prompts follow your keyboard layout**, even when you switch it while playing.
- **Mouse on mounted guns:** the Uncharted 1 jeep gun and the turrets of all three games turn with the mouse.
- **Saves:**
  - The game's own save list, with a picture of each save taken where it was made; Delete / Triangle (Y)
    twice deletes one.
  - **Saves page** in the launcher: every save with its picture; delete, restore, or delete for good.
  - **Backups:** before every start each changed save is copied (the last 5 are kept); Saves → Backups puts
    one back.
- **Screenshots:** **F12** in the game saves a full-size picture (without El Dorado's menu or counter); the
  **Gallery** page shows them per game.
- **Trophies page:** your progress and every trophy of each game, read from your own game files.
- **Discord:** your profile shows *Playing El Dorado* with the game, the chapter of your latest save and the
  time played (set `UNCHARTED_DISCORD=0` to turn it off).
- Desktop shortcuts per game with that game's icon (made from your own game files); its own settings and
  updates.

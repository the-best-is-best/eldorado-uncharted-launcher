**El Dorado Launcher** for **Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** on PC. **Beta 2.**

The launcher offers this update by itself (Settings → LAUNCHER → Check for updates). New players: extract the zip
anywhere, start **Uncharted Launcher.exe** and choose your game folder (the one with eboot.bin and sce_sys).
No game files are included: you play your own copy (update 1.02 recommended).

## Highlights

- 🪟 **The window fits your screen:** playing in a window at your screen's own size no longer goes past the screen
  and under the taskbar; the window takes the usable area, keeping its shape.
- ⌨️ **F11 switches between a window and fullscreen** while you play (for that session; Settings → DISPLAY keeps
  your start setting).
- 🎬 **Cutscenes no longer stick on one picture** while their sound goes on (seen on 8 GB cards at 1440p after
  texture swaps).
- 💥 **When a game crashes or freezes**, the launcher shows why, with **PLAY AGAIN** (back to your last save) and
  **PROBLEM REPORT**. This works for games started from Steam or a Desktop shortcut too; a normal close still
  takes you straight back.
- 🎮 **Add the three games to Steam**, now each with El Dorado's own icon and library pictures (banner, title and
  covers) in its color. If Steam is open, the launcher closes it, adds the games and opens it again.
- 📈 **Smoother play, measured on your PC:** after a session that ran clearly below your frame rate limit
  because the graphics card held it back, the launcher offers one step lower upscaling quality. Nothing changes
  without your yes.
- ⚡ The launcher's buttons work right away at start (the update check no longer holds them back).
- ⌨️ The number pad's **Del** deletes a save like **Delete** does, with Num Lock on or off.
- ⏸️ **The game stands still in the background:** minimized or Alt+Tabbed, everything stops (time, enemies,
  sound), and coming back opens the game's pause menu.
- 🚪 Uncharted 1's turn-the-wheel prompt on the keyboard wears the launcher's look in the game's color.
- 💰 **Give all treasures** now works once per save: a new playthrough gets them too.

Everything from Beta 1 stays: see its notes for the whole list.

## Known issues

- Uncharted 1: one crash was seen once when entering the jungle after the temple (the game's own memory heap);
  it did not happen again. If you hit it, please send the Problem report (Settings → LAUNCHER).
- The in-game menu shows Korean and Traditional Chinese in English (its font has none of their characters).

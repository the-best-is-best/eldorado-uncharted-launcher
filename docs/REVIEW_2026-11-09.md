# Review: starting, loading, saving and the shader cache (9 Nov 2026)

Read-only review of the code and of the real logs of the test install (`El Dorado (test)\analysis`), after the merge
of Mevlock 0.3.6.50. Numbers are measured unless marked *guess*. Nothing here is changed yet.

## Start: from PLAY to the first picture

Usually 15.7-19.0 s for Uncharted 1 ("Loading screen: the game shows its first picture after N s").

| # | What | Evidence | Change | Gain | Risk | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | The game freezes while its window is not in front, also while it loads | U1 frozen 12.5 s during loading (28.8 s start), U3 33.3 s (46.1 s start); the U3 "hang after choosing a difficulty" was this too: 10.5 s and 12.0 s with the window in the background (a screen recorder takes the focus) | No freeze before the first picture, and on the preparation page | 10-33 s in those runs | low | small |
| S2 | The update view (4,913 links, 67 junctions) is deleted and rebuilt at every start | build 1.5-2.6 s, delete 0.5-3.4 s (`tools/game_update_view.py:105`) | Keep it while a stamp of the game and update folders is the same | 2-6 s every start | low-medium | small |
| S3 | The same listed shader variants are rebuilt at every start | U1 "266-275 listed variants not in the cache" on 13+ starts in a row; U3 1,770 every start, the game waits 2.1 s | Log why a variant is not found, then key it right | U3 ~2 s, U1 ~0.4 s, U2 maybe ~10 s | medium | medium |
| S4 | The text languages of the three games are read on a cache miss | 6.3 s (u1 1.2, u2 2.2, u3 2.9) on first start / after a pkg install | Fill the cache in the background in the launcher | 6.3 s on those starts | low | small |
| S5 | `run.py` re-prepares a game whenever any `engine/scripts/*.py` changed | 0.3-0.6 s, after updates only | Depend on prepare / link_modules / patches only | small | low | small |
| S6 | Extra Python processes and repeated hashing of the executables | ~0.2 s + ~0.1 s warm | Import instead of start; hash once | ~0.3 s | low | small |

The rest of the ~17 s is the game's own front-end load with no log lines; timestamps on each boot.log line
(`tools/probe_uncharted.py`, the stdout loop) would split it.

## Shader cache

| # | What | Evidence | Change | Gain | Risk | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | One small file per blob (create .tmp, write, rename) | 33,234 files, 663 MB; writing ~500 files/s against ~1,600/s made; all 4 writers stall together 1.3-2.9 s | One append-only pack per game, written in large batches | the write backlog mostly gone; the new wait on the preparation page (~50 s for U3 after an update, *estimate*) shrinks with it | medium (new format, Mevlock merges) | large |
| C2 | All three games share one cache folder (named by CUSA02320) | every game lists all 33k files; a profile mismatch clears all three | One subfolder per game | faster listing, no cross-game clears | low | small |
| C3 | Expected misses logged as errors on a first preparation | 10,614 "Failed to open" lines for U3, log 3 MB | List the names once, skip missing ones quietly | some 100 ms, smaller logs | none | small |
| C4 | The "prepared" stamp is the list's file size | a list change shows the full page even when 98% is cached | Stamp with a hash; skip the page when few are missing | seconds after small list updates | low | small |

The zip ("archived") mode is not an answer as written: one writer, best compression, a single preload thread, index
written only at close.

## Saves

| # | What | Evidence | Change | Gain | Risk | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Deleting or restoring a save moves the engine's backup from an old folder (`.backup`); Mevlock 0.3.6.49 keeps it in `<title>.backups/auto` | `launcher/core/saves.py:259,310`, `runtime_savedata.c:194` | Move `.backups/auto/<dir>` with the save | closes a rare case where a crash repair puts another save's data back | low | small |
| V2 | Save pictures are written for the profile and system saves too | 310 of 562 pictures (SAVEDATA01P, 0CC), 1.3 MB each, encoded on the save thread | Pictures only for the campaign saves | half the PNG writes | none | small |
| V3 | Every save close re-reads the whole save and forces a disk write, even read-only | 181 of 846 mounts read-only; `.closed` rewritten with the same 20 bytes | Skip for read-only mounts; write `.closed` only when it changed | less disk work at each save | low | small |
| V4 | The launcher's backups compare names, sizes and times | 2 of 31 kept backups are identical copies | Compare a hash of the save data | no wasted backup slots | low | small |
| V5 | Pictures are 77% of save storage (960x540 PNG, 1.1-1.3 MB) | ~65 MB in all | Smaller or one picture per backup | disk only | low | small |
| V6 | The save list decodes every full picture on the game's thread before opening | *guess* 100-200 ms with 10 saves | A small copy next to it, or decode in the background | faster list | low | medium |

## Recommended order

1. S1 (no freeze while loading), V1 (backup folder), S2 (keep the update view), V2 + V3 + C3: all small, together
   about 2-6 s off every start and up to tens of seconds when focus is lost while loading.
2. C2 (a cache folder per game), S4, S5, S6, V4: small, less frequent gains.
3. S3 (variants rebuilt every start): medium, needs one logging round first.
4. C1 (cache pack): large; do it after the small ones, and measure the preparation page wait first.

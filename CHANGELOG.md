# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- **Fast engine:** draws prepared on several threads, a profile-optimized build (the Uncharted 1 jungle at
  about 52 FPS on an RTX 3060 Laptop); shaders prepared once per game.
- **New launcher:** one window with Games, Saves, Trophies, Gallery and Settings, in the series' look (leather,
  parchment, gold); where you left off in each game, play time, last play, average FPS and trophy progress;
  keyboard, mouse or controller, with the key hints of the device you use.
- **Recommended preset** picked from your GPU on the first start; DLSS, FSR 3.1, FSR 4, **frame generation**.
- **In-game menu** (Insert, L3 + R3 or L2 + R3) with FPS, CPU, GPU, RAM and VRAM; **Quit** in the games' menus.
- **Key prompts** follow the keyboard layout live; **mouse** on the jeep gun and all mounted guns.
- **Saves:** the game's save list with pictures and delete; the Saves page with delete, restore and delete
  for good; **backups** before every start, with restore.
- **F12 screenshots** and a **Gallery** page; a **Trophies** page; **Discord** presence.
- Desktop shortcuts per game with that game's icon (made from your own game files); its own settings and
  updates.
